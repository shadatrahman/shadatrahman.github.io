> Any fool can know. The point is to understand.
{: .prompt-info }

— **Albert Einstein**

Einstein's words cut to the heart of what it means to truly learn. In an age where information is abundant, the real challenge isn't acquiring facts—it's developing deep understanding. Knowledge is knowing that a tomato is a fruit; wisdom is knowing not to put it in a fruit salad. But understanding? That's knowing why it works the way it does.
